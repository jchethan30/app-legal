TufFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

TufFit is a workout tracker made by Chethan Jayaramu ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. The short version: your training data stays on your phone unless you choose to sign in, we never sell it, and there are no ads or tracking.

Contact: infotuffit@gmail.com

1. Data that stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored on your device:

  • Workouts, sets, exercises, templates, programs, goals and personal records
  • Your profile (name, sex, when you started lifting, experience level) and profile photo
  • Bodyweight entries, readiness check-ins (sleep, energy, soreness) and injuries you log
  • Custom exercises and their photos and videos, gyms, settings and badges

Some of this is health information: your bodyweight, injuries, readiness check-ins and sex. We cannot see any of it unless you create an account (section 2).

Your data is included in your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) if you have that turned on; those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies.

You decide whether anything else leaves your phone:

  • Export sends a file only where you choose to share it.
  • Send feedback opens your own email app with a message to us. Besides what you write, it includes the app version, build number, phone model and operating system version, so we can look into problems. You can read and edit it before sending.

2. If you create an account (optional)

Signing in is optional. Everything works without an account. Before you sign in, the app asks for your explicit consent to store your training data, including the health information above, on our servers. Without that consent nothing is uploaded.

If you sign in, we store:

  • Your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code (no password). The code is emailed to you by our provider Supabase.
  • A copy of your training data from section 1, so it can sync between your devices. Photos and videos are not uploaded; they stay on the phone that has them.

This data is stored by our provider Supabase, on servers in Singapore. Each account can only read and write its own data. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and your sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage.

To keep the service secure and working, Supabase also keeps technical logs of sign-ins and requests to our server. These include your IP address and basic device and app details. They are kept for no more than 7 days (currently 1 day) and then deleted automatically.

You can withdraw your consent at any time: sign out to stop syncing, and delete your account (Settings → Account) to remove your data from our servers.

3. Crash reports

Beta and release versions of the app send a report to our provider Sentry (servers in the United States) when the app crashes or hits an error. A report contains the error, where in the app it happened, the app version, the phone model and the operating system version.

Before a report is sent, the app removes anything that could identify you, and Sentry is set not to store IP addresses. Crash reports do not contain your name, email, IP address, workouts, screenshots or anything you typed. We use them only to find and fix bugs, and Sentry deletes them after 30 days.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't show ads and don't use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't use your data to make decisions about you, such as for insurance or employment.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Photos: only when you choose a profile photo, or a photo or video for a custom exercise. It is copied into the app and stays on your phone.
  • Notifications: for rest-timer alerts. These are created on your phone; nothing is sent to a server.

6. How long we keep data

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Account data stays until you delete your account. Items you delete in the app are removed from the server too (after 30 days in Recently deleted, or straight away if you delete them permanently).
  • When you delete your account, your data is removed from our database straight away. Copies in our provider's backups are removed when those backups expire.
  • Technical logs: no more than 7 days. Crash reports: 30 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account: your training data never leaves your phone without an account.
  • Access and export: export your workouts from the app at any time.
  • Correct: edit any of your data in the app.
  • Withdraw consent and delete: sign out to stop syncing; delete individual items in the app, or delete your whole account in Settings → Account → Delete account. You can also email us to ask for deletion.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, Singapore's PDPA, or California's CCPA), you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to your local data protection authority. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Legal basis (EU/UK users):

  • Health information (bodyweight, injuries, readiness check-ins, sex) stored with your account: your explicit consent, given in the app before sign-in. You can withdraw it at any time as described above; this doesn't affect processing before you withdrew it.
  • Your email address and the rest of your account data: to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • Crash reports and technical logs: our legitimate interest in keeping the app secure and working.

8. International transfers

If you sign in, your data is transferred to Singapore (Supabase); crash reports go to the United States (Sentry). These transfers are covered by our data processing agreements with those providers, which include the standard contractual clauses approved for transfers from the EU and UK.

9. Children

TufFit is not intended for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU/UK), and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect. If a change affects what you consented to, the app asks for your consent again.

11. Contact
